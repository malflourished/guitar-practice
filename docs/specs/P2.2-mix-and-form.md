# P2.2: mix faders and the form strip

**Package:** P2.2 in `docs/ROADMAP.md`, after P2 and P2.1 (both merged).
**Status:** spec written 2026-11-13.

## Why

The owner's second play-along (2026-11-13):
- On the mix: "the bass sounds a little soft still, the pads sound maybe a little loud, so having some control over that would be nice."
- On the form: at lower tempos, chords that last several bars made the owner think "we were just stuck in a loop", unsure "if the progression was going to keep happening".

The 12-bar blues holds I7 for four bars, which is 16 seconds at 60 BPM, and nothing on screen says where the player is. The fix is to make the form visible and audible, not to shorten it. Keeping your place through four bars of one chord is part of what the blues teaches.

## 1. Three faders

- Three small vertical faders labelled **Guitar**, **Bass** and **Pad**, at the right end of the Progressions deck. They show whenever Progressions is open, so the mix can be set before Play.
- Range: off, then −24 dB to +6 dB around today's default, where 0 dB is the current default level. While dragging or hovering, show the value ("−3 dB", "off"). Double-click resets to 0 dB.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move 1 dB, Shift+arrow 3 dB. Use `aria-valuetext` in dB.
- A change applies at once while the loop plays, ramped over about 30 ms, with no restart.
- Saved with the other Play options, for the player rather than per entry.
- Look: the app's glass style, minimal. A thin track, a small cap, labels in the small-caps label style. It must work in both themes.

**Engine.** Give the jam three gain nodes, one each for guitar, bass and pad, feeding the existing output chain:
- `JAM_GUITAR_LEVEL` becomes the guitar fader's 0 dB.
- The `BassVoice` and `PadVoice` default volumes stay as they are, and their faders scale them.
- With a fader at off, skip scheduling that layer's events.

**Play menu.**
- The faders replace the Off choices. Guitar becomes Whole, Half or Quarter, and the Pad group goes.
- Migrate saved options: Guitar `'off'` becomes the Guitar fader at off with the rhythm at Half; Pad off becomes the Pad fader at off.
- The menu keeps Guitar, Feel, Click, Loop and "Move along the neck", in that order.

## 2. The form strip

- The deck shows the selected progression as a lead sheet: one cell per bar, four bars to a line. The 12-bar blues reads as three lines of four, the way it is taught, and a five-bar entry reads four and one.
- Each cell shows the chord symbol (C7) with its Roman numeral (I⁷) small underneath. When a chord repeats, it is written again in a dimmer tone, so the changes stand out.
- While playing, the current cell is lit and a thin playhead crosses it with the beat. The beat dots stay.
- From the second pass on, a small "pass 2" appears.
- The strip replaces the "bar n / N" text.
- When stopped, clicking a cell selects that step, as the stepper does. While playing, cells do nothing.

## 3. The next change

While playing, beside the current chord, name the next different chord and how far away it is: "F7 in 3 bars", then "F7 in 2 bars", then "F7 next bar". On the last beat before a change (`changeComing`), the next chord's name brightens.

Add a pure helper beside `jamPosition`: `nextChange(stepIndex, chords)` returns `{ stepIndex, bars }` for the next step whose chord differs, wrapping round the loop, or `null` for a one-chord loop.

## 4. The top of the form

From the second pass on, a soft cymbal plays on beat 1 of bar 1, as a drummer marks the top of the form. Use white noise through a high-pass at about 6 kHz, with a decay of about 1.5 s, quiet: about 12 dB under the guitar's peak. It has no fader of its own and plays whether or not Click is on. If the owner finds it too much, a toggle comes later.

## Tests

- `nextChange` over the 12-bar blues (from bar 1 the next change is F7 in 4 bars, from bar 12 it is C7 next bar, wrapping) and a four-bar loop. A one-chord loop gives `null`.
- Fader mapping: 0 dB gives a gain of 1, off gives 0, and +6 dB gives about 2. Saved values outside the range are clamped.
- The form-strip layout helper: 12 bars give lines of [4, 4, 4], 5 bars give [4, 1], 4 bars give [4].
- Play-options migration, as above.

## Done when

- [x] The three faders set the guitar, bass and pad levels live, are saved, and reset on double-click. The Play menu has no Off choices.
- [x] The form strip shows the progression four bars to a line, lights the current bar and dims repeats. Selecting a bar works when stopped.
- [x] The next change and the bars until it show while playing.
- [x] A soft cymbal marks the top of each pass from the second pass on.
- [x] No page scrolls at 1440×900, 1280×720 or 960×640. Screenshots of the deck at all three sizes, playing, in both themes.
- [x] Tests, lint and `tsc` pass.
- [ ] The owner sets a mix they like and never wonders whether the loop is stuck.

## Out of scope here

- New instrument sounds: the modelled bass is package S, running beside this one. Keep `BassVoice` and `PadVoice` as they are, apart from routing them through the new gain nodes.
- Drums beyond the one cymbal.
- Speeding up each pass, or moving key each pass round the circle: discussed with the owner for later.
